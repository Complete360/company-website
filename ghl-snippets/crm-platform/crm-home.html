Questions before you switch

Pricing, trial and billing questions are answered over on the pricing page.

What does this actually replace?

For most of our clients: their CRM, their booking tool, their email marketing platform, their business texting app, their review-request software, and their invoicing. Some keep one specialist tool — accounting, or field-service dispatch — and connect it.

The goal isn't to replace everything you own. It's to stop leads falling into the gaps between the things you own.

Will it work for my industry?

It's in daily use across home services, trades, insurance and financial agents, real estate, medical and dental practices, and legal firms. The platform is the same underneath; what changes is how the pipeline stages, intake forms and follow-up sequences are configured for how your business actually sells.

That configuration happens during onboarding, with you, not after you're handed a login.

Can I keep my current business phone number?

In most cases yes — existing business numbers can be ported in so calls and texts route through the platform without your customers noticing a change.

If you'd rather not move your main line, you can run a new number alongside it and keep the old one exactly as it is. Plenty of clients start that way and port later.

What happens to the contacts I already have?

They come with you. Export from your current tool as a CSV and we import them during onboarding, mapping tags and fields across where the source data supports it.

A messy export is normal and not a problem — cleaning it up is part of the setup work.

Do I own my data?

Yes. Your contacts and conversation history belong to you and can be exported at any time, including if you decide to leave.

Do I need to be technical to run it?

No. The parts most owners touch daily are the mobile app and the pipeline board, and both are drag-and-tap. The heavier machinery — automations, workflows, campaign builds — we set up with you and you edit later if you want to.

Can my whole team use it?

Every plan includes seats — two on Starter, five on Unlimited, unlimited on Enterprise — with per-user permissions, so a technician sees their jobs without seeing your revenue reporting.

How is this different from hiring you to run our ads?

Advertising creates leads. The CRM catches and converts them. They're separate purchases and either one works on its own — but ad spend goes a lot further when the leads it generates get followed up automatically instead of sitting in an inbox.
