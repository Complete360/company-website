Everything you need to know about geo-fencing advertising with Complete360.

Geo-fencing advertising lets you draw a virtual boundary — a "fence" — around any physical location, like a competitor's business, a trade show, a neighborhood, or any area where your ideal customers spend time. When someone enters that zone with their mobile device, they start seeing your ads on the apps and websites they use throughout the day. It's hyper-local targeting that reaches real people in real places, not just cookie-based audiences online.
Most digital ads target based on browsing behavior, demographics, or interests — which is broad and imprecise. Geo-fencing targets based on physical location, meaning you're reaching people who were actually present in a specific place. This makes it uniquely powerful for local businesses — you can target people who visited a competitor, attended a local event, or live in a specific zip code. The foot traffic attribution feature also lets you track whether someone who saw your ad actually walked into your business.
