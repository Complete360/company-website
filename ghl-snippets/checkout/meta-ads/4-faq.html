Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Facebook & Instagram ad management before signing up.

What's the difference between my management fee and my ad budget?+

They're two separate costs. Your management fee — the plan you select here — is paid to Complete360 for building, running, and optimizing your campaigns. Your ad budget is paid directly to Meta and funds the actual ads; we recommend a minimum of $600/mo for effective local lead generation. You always own and control your ad account, and we never mark up your ad spend.

Is there a setup fee?+

Yes — a one-time $499 setup fee applies to every plan. It covers onboarding, ad-account and tracking setup, audience research, and building your first campaign. It's a separate one-time charge from your monthly (or prepaid) management fee, and it's shown on your order before you pay.

What access do you need to run my ads?+

We need access to your Meta ad account through Meta Business Manager — or we'll help you create one during onboarding. This lets us build and manage campaigns on your behalf. You retain full ownership of the ad account and your billing at all times; we only use the access to run and optimize your campaigns.

Do you create the ads, or do I?+

We handle all of it — strategy, ad copy, creative design, and lead forms. During onboarding you tell us about your business, services, and offers, and from there our team builds everything. You'll review and approve the direction, but you won't be stuck making ads. If you have brand assets or photos you'd like us to use, we'll happily work them in.

How soon after signing up will my campaigns launch?+

Most campaigns launch within a few business days of completing onboarding. We'll send you a simple intake form right after checkout to collect your business details, ad-account access, and offers. Meta's system then uses the early period to learn who converts best, so performance and cost per lead keep improving as we optimize.

Where do my leads go?+

Straight into your Complete360 CRM, so you can follow up instantly by text, email, or call. Fast follow-up is one of the biggest factors in turning a Meta lead into a paying customer, and the platform makes it automatic — every call and form lead is tracked in one place.

How do I cancel?+

The Month-to-Month plan has no long-term contract — to cancel, send us written notice at least 10 days before your next billing date. The 6-Month and 12-Month plans are paid upfront for a lower effective rate and, like the one-time $499 setup fee, are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
