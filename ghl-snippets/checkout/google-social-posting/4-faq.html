Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Google Social Posting service before getting started.

What exactly is a Google Post?+

Google Posts are short updates that appear directly on your Google Business Profile in Search and Maps — similar to social media posts but on Google itself. They can highlight an offer, an event, a product, or general news. Posting consistently signals to Google that your business is active, which helps your local ranking and gives searchers another reason to choose you over a competitor who hasn't posted in months.

Do I have to write or approve the posts?+

No — it's fully done for you. During onboarding you tell us about your business, services, and any recurring offers, and from there our team handles the writing and design every week. If you'd like to review posts before they go live or send us specific promotions to feature, just let your account contact know and we'll work it into the schedule.

How many posts will I get each month?+

You'll get roughly 4–5 posts per month — a consistent weekly cadence, which is the sweet spot Google looks for. Each post includes optimized copy, a custom branded graphic, and an action button. We rotate through different post types (What's New, Offers, Events, Products) so your profile never looks repetitive.

Do I need an existing Google Business Profile?+

Yes — posting requires a verified Google Business Profile that we can access to publish on your behalf. If you don't have one yet, we offer a one-time GBP Setup for $399 to get you verified and ready, then posting can begin right away.

How is this different from full GBP Management?+

This service is focused purely on posting — we write, design, and publish weekly Google Posts for you. Full GBP Management does that and a lot more: review generation and responses, profile optimization, citation and AI-platform sync through Yext, and monthly rank reporting. Google Posting is a great fit if you already have a profile in good shape and just need the posting handled consistently. You can step up to full management anytime.

Can I cancel or change plans later?+

The Month-to-Month plan can be cancelled anytime with 30 days' notice. The 6-Month and 12-Month plans are prepaid for the term at a lower monthly rate. You can always upgrade to a longer term or step up to full GBP Management whenever it makes sense — just reach out to your account manager.

How long until I start seeing results?+

Most clients see improved profile engagement (more views, clicks, and calls) within the first 60–90 days of consistent posting. Google rewards activity over time — the longer you post consistently, the more your profile is favored in local search results. This is why the 6 and 12-month plans tend to produce the strongest results.
