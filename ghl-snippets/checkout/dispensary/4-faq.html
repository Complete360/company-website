Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about geo-fencing advertising for cannabis dispensaries.

Is geo-fencing advertising legal for cannabis businesses?+

Yes. Geo-fencing on cannabis-compliant programmatic networks is fully legal and the most effective digital advertising option available to dispensaries. Unlike Google Ads or Meta, our platform is built specifically for cannabis businesses — delivering to 21+ verified audiences without violating any ad platform policies.

How does geo-fencing work without Google or Meta platforms?+

We use a cannabis-compliant programmatic ad network that reaches audiences across thousands of apps, websites, and streaming TV platforms — completely separate from Google and Meta. Your ads are delivered to users based on their real-world location data, reaching them on their phones and TV screens wherever they go after entering your geo-fenced zones.

What ad formats are included in the packages?+

The 200k and 400k packages include mobile banner ads and CTV (connected TV) video ads. The 600k Market Domination package adds desktop display. The VFW / Legion Starter is mobile banner only. All creative — banners and video — is produced by our in-house team and included in the management fee.

Can I target my competitors' customers?+

Absolutely. Competitor geo-fencing is included in the 200k package and above. We draw a virtual fence around competing dispensaries and target customers who visit those locations — serving your ads across their devices after they leave. This is one of the most powerful customer acquisition tactics available to dispensaries.

How long does it take to launch a campaign?+

Most campaigns go live within 5–7 business days of onboarding. That includes your strategy call, geo-fence zone setup, creative production, compliance review, and campaign activation. Nothing launches without your approval on the creative first.

What is the VFW / Legion Starter package?+

The VFW / Legion Starter is a specialized veteran outreach package designed to connect cannabis dispensaries with the veteran community — a high-intent, cannabis-friendly audience. We geo-fence the 5 closest American Legion and VFW posts to your location and serve 21+ verified mobile banner ads to that audience. It's also part of our Home Town Hero Partners program.

What does the monthly vs. annual pricing difference mean?+

The annual price is paid upfront for the full year and reflects our best rate. The month-to-month option gives you flexibility without a long-term commitment but is priced slightly higher per month. Both options include the same campaign, impressions, and deliverables — the difference is simply billing frequency and total cost. Month-to-month plans need at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date to cancel, and annual plans are non-refundable from the time of purchase.

Is the ad spend separate from the management fee?+

Yes. The package price covers campaign management, creative production, zone setup, reporting, and account management. Ad spend — the budget that actually funds your impressions — is a separate cost that goes directly to the network. We'll walk you through the recommended ad spend on your strategy call based on your package and market size.
