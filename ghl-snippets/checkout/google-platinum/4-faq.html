Common questions before signing up — answered straight.

The Platinum Package bundles three of our Google services — Local Service Ads management, Google Business Profile management, and Reputation Management — into one plan managed by one team. Instead of paying separately for each service and coordinating across different providers, you get your entire Google presence handled under one roof with a single point of contact and a combined performance dashboard.
Because LSA, your Google Business Profile, and reviews are not independent — they compound each other. Google reviews that come in through your Google Business Profile directly influence your LSA ranking, meaning more reviews equals better ad placement and lower cost per lead. At the same time, your LSA ads drive calls that lead to more reviews. When the same team manages all three, they can optimize this feedback loop intentionally rather than treating each channel in isolation. That's the core advantage of the Platinum Package.
When you sign up for LSA as a standalone service, the $499 setup fee covers the time and work required to build out your account from scratch. When you bundle it into the Platinum Package, we're doing all of that work anyway as part of the broader onboarding — so we absorb the setup cost. It's our way of rewarding clients who commit to the full Google presence approach rather than piecemealing it together over time.
Google Business Profile management and reputation management can begin within days of onboarding. LSA has the longest lead time due to Google's verification process — typically 1 to 3 weeks depending on how quickly your background check and documents are approved. We run all three onboarding tracks simultaneously so nothing waits on anything else. Most clients are fully live across all three channels within 2 to 3 weeks of signing up.
Reputation management covers review response management — we respond to new Google reviews on your behalf professionally and promptly — plus an active review generation strategy to consistently grow your review count across platforms. We also sync your review profile across key platforms and track your overall reputation score as part of the combined performance dashboard.

We'll recommend a starting budget based on your market and industry during onboarding, and adjust it monthly based on performance. You stay in full control of the ad spend at all times.
The monthly plan has no long-term contract — to cancel, send us written notice at least 10 days before your next billing date. Quarterly, semi-annual, and annual plans are paid upfront in exchange for the discounted rate and are non-refundable from the time of purchase. Since all three services are actively being managed from day one, the work investment starts immediately at onboarding.
