Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI Voice Agent, AI Assistant, and Custom Agents before signing up.

What's the difference between AI Voice Agent and AI Assistant?+

AI Voice Agent handles inbound phone calls 24/7 — answering, qualifying, and booking appointments over the phone. AI Assistant includes everything in Voice Agent plus website & SMS chat, automated review requests, and custom workflow automation, so one AI Assistant runs your entire front office across every channel.

Is there a setup fee?+

Yes — both plans include a one-time $2,000 setup fee. This covers training your AI Agent on your business, provisioning your dedicated phone number, and integrating your calendar and CRM. The setup fee is a one-time cost and is not refundable.

Do you charge per call or per minute?+

No per-minute AI fees — AI usage is unlimited on every plan: every minute the agent talks, every message it sends, every review it answers. Your phone line and carrier usage (the cost of the call itself) are billed separately, just like any business phone service. We'll walk you through expected usage during onboarding so there are no surprises.

Are there any costs besides my monthly plan?+

Yes, two things sit outside the monthly plan fee: the one-time $2,000 setup fee, and your phone line and carrier usage, which are billed separately. Both are covered in detail during onboarding before your agent goes live.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?+

Yes — reach out to your account manager to switch. Moving to annual billing locks in two months free compared to paying monthly.

What happens if I cancel my annual plan?+

Annual plans are billed once per year and aren't refunded or prorated. To stop an annual plan from renewing, let us know in writing at least 10 days before the renewal date. Monthly plans can be cancelled with written notice at least 10 days before your next billing date.

How soon will my AI Agent be live?+

Most AI Agents are live within 5–7 business days of completing onboarding. We'll send an intake form right after checkout to collect your business details, call flows, and calendar/CRM access.

Can I add Custom Agents features later?+

Yes — you can upgrade from AI Voice Agent to AI Assistant, or add Custom Agents workflows, at any time. Just reach out to your account manager and we'll scope the change.
