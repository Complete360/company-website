CTV / OTT advertising

Everything you need to know before launching your CTV campaign.

CTV stands for Connected TV — any television that connects to the internet, including smart TVs, Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, and game consoles. OTT (Over-The-Top) refers to streaming content delivered over the internet rather than cable. CTV/OTT advertising places your 15s or 30s non-skippable video ad in front of viewers watching streaming content on their television screens. It combines the reach and impact of TV advertising with the precise targeting of digital — including geo-fence targeting so you only reach people in your local market.
Mobile geo-fencing ads are banner ads that appear on phones and tablets as people browse apps and websites. CTV ads are full-screen video ads that play on TV screens during streaming content — think Hulu, Peacock, Pluto TV, and thousands of other streaming apps. CTV ads are non-skippable and command much higher attention than mobile banners. Many businesses run both simultaneously: mobile banners for ongoing awareness and CTV ads for high-impact brand moments on the big screen.
