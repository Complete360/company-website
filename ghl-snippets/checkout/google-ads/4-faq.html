Quick answers

A one-time $499 setup fee covers your campaign build, conversion and call tracking, and launch. There's no long-term contract on the monthly plan — to cancel, just send us written notice at least 10 days before your next billing date. Our 6- and 12-month plans are paid upfront to lock in a lower rate, and like all our services they're non-refundable from the time of purchase.

Two separate costs. Your management fee (starting at $399/mo) is paid to Complete360 for building and running your campaigns. Your ad budget is paid directly to Google and funds the actual clicks. You always own and control your Google Ads account.

For local search, we recommend starting at a minimum of $1,000/mo in ad spend, paid directly to Google. Clicks cost more on Search because the intent is higher — people are looking for your service right now. We'll recommend a specific budget for your market on your strategy call.

Prepay plans are a single upfront payment — they don't auto-renew, so you'll never get a surprise charge. As your term nears its end, we'll reach out to renew so your campaigns keep running without a gap.

Faster than most channels. Because Google captures people already searching for what you offer, campaigns can start producing calls within days of going live. From there we optimize continuously, so your cost per lead keeps improving over the first several weeks.
