Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting a website built with Complete360.

How long does it take to build my website? +

Most websites are completed within 2–3 weeks from your kickoff strategy call. Timeline depends on how quickly we receive your content (photos, copy, logo) and revision turnaround. We'll keep you updated at every step and won't launch without your final approval.

Can I make changes to my site myself? +

Yes — your website is built on a platform that allows us to make changes on your behalf as part of your monthly plan. If you'd like to make edits yourself, we can walk you through the basics. Most clients simply send us change requests and we handle them — usually within 1–2 business days.

Can I use Complete360 to redesign my existing site? +

Absolutely. If you already have a website, we'll review it during your strategy call, identify what's working and what isn't, and build you a new site from scratch on our platform. Your old site stays live until the new one is ready to launch — zero downtime.

What if I already have a domain name? +

No problem — we'll connect your existing domain to your new Complete360 website. We'll walk you through the DNS update process (or do it with you). If you don't have a domain yet, we'll help you choose and register one.

What does the monthly fee cover? +

The monthly fee covers secure managed hosting, SSL certificate, platform updates, ongoing maintenance, and access to your account manager for content changes and support. Essentials and Pro are $97/mo. Premium is $197/mo and includes priority support and more frequent updates.

Do the templates look the same way for every business? +

No — every site is customized to your brand. We start from an industry-ready template (colors, layout, structure) and then apply your logo, brand colors, photos, and content. The result looks and feels unique to your business, not like a generic template.

What custom options are available for my build? +

We can build pretty much anything — booking systems, lead capture forms, chat widgets, photo galleries, service area pages, pricing pages, video sections, and more. Pro and Premium plans have more room for custom features. If you have something specific in mind, just let us know during your strategy call and we'll let you know what's included vs. what would be an add-on.

What happens if I need to cancel my monthly plan? +

There's no long-term contract. To cancel, send us written notice at least 10 days before your next billing date. If you cancel, your site will stay live until the end of the billing period. You retain rights to all the content we created for you.
