Why NAP Consistency Matters

The silent ranking factor
most businesses ignore.

NAP stands for Name, Address, and Phone number. Google and every major AI search engine use this data to verify and rank local businesses. Inconsistencies across the web silently destroy your ranking — even if your Google profile looks perfect.

Local Pack Rankings
Google's algorithm cross-references your business data across hundreds of sources. Consistent NAP signals authority and accuracy — driving your business higher in the Local Pack where buyers click first.
AI Citation Eligibility
AI search engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity rely on verified, consistent directory data to recommend businesses. Without accurate listings across the web, AI won't recommend you — regardless of your reviews.
Duplicate Suppression
Old addresses, name variations, and auto-generated duplicates from years past can actively suppress your real listing. We identify and eliminate these conflicting entries so only your correct, verified data wins.
Voice & Mobile Search
When someone asks Siri, Alexa, or Google Assistant to call a local business, voice results pull from verified listing data. If your phone number is wrong on even one directory, that call goes to a competitor.
Instant Holiday & Hour Updates
Change your hours for a holiday or seasonal shift and it propagates to every platform automatically. No more customers showing up when you're closed because one directory hadn't been updated in three years.
Visibility Analytics
