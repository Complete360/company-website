ChatGPT Ads questions

What are ChatGPT Ads?

They're ads from OpenAI that appear inside ChatGPT, below the answer, labeled as sponsored. They're matched to the topic of the conversation, so a business can show up when someone is asking about the kind of problem it solves.

Do ChatGPT Ads change what ChatGPT recommends?

No. OpenAI says ads don't influence ChatGPT's answers, and advertisers can't see people's conversations. Your ad shows up beside the answer, not inside it.

Who sees ChatGPT Ads?

People using ChatGPT's Free and Go plans, and people using ChatGPT without logging in (who only see ads suitable for all ages). People on paid plans like Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu don't see ads, and ChatGPT doesn't show ads to accounts identified as under 18.

Are ChatGPT Ads right for my business?

They tend to fit businesses whose customers research before they buy, especially for higher-value jobs. If most of your work comes from urgent calls, Local Services Ads and answering every call usually come first. We'll give you an honest recommendation on a strategy call.

How much do ChatGPT Ads cost?

There are two parts. Our management fee is set with you on a strategy call. Your ad budget is separate and paid directly to OpenAI, and you choose how much to start with. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.

Can you guarantee leads from ChatGPT Ads?

No. ChatGPT Ads is a new channel, and no one can promise a set number of leads or results. What we do promise is careful setup, honest reporting and steady improvements to your campaigns each month.

Can I run ChatGPT Ads with my other marketing?

Yes, and we recommend it. ChatGPT Ads work best alongside a strong Google Business Profile, Google PPC and Local Services Ads, so customers find you wherever they look.
