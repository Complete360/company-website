How Geo-Fencing Advertising Works

We use real-world location behavior data to build hyper-accurate audience segments — then deliver your ads to the right screens at exactly the right moment.

1

Define Your Fence

We draw a virtual boundary around locations your target audience visits — competitor stores, events, neighborhoods, or any real-world area.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about geo-fencing advertising with Complete360.

What exactly is geo-fencing advertising?+

Geo-fencing lets you draw a virtual boundary around a real-world location. When someone enters that boundary with their mobile device, their device ID is captured and they start receiving your ads across mobile apps, websites, and connected TV screens.

How is geo-fencing different from regular digital ads?+

Traditional ads target demographics, interests, or browsing behavior. Geo-fencing targets based on physical real-world location — you're reaching people who have actually been to specific places. Far more accurate for local businesses where geography matters.

How long does it take to launch a campaign?+

Most campaigns go live within 5–7 business days of onboarding. This includes defining your geo-fences, building your audience, getting ad creatives approved, and activating the campaign. We handle the entire setup process for you.

Do I need to provide my own ad creatives?+

Not necessarily. We work with your existing brand assets and provide creative guidance. Full creative development is included in our Pro package. All creatives go through a quality review before launch — nothing goes live without your approval.

Can I target my competitors' customers?+

Yes — this is one of the most powerful applications. We can draw a fence around a competitor's location and serve your ads to people who have recently visited there. Highly effective for contractors, insurance agents, real estate professionals, and more.

How do the commitment pricing tiers work?+

There are three options — all include the exact same campaign and 8,000 impressions per month. Month-to-month is $299/mo billed monthly. The 6-month package is $1,435.20 paid in full (20% off, $239.20/mo effective). The 12-month package is $2,511.60 paid in full (30% off, $209.30/mo effective). Unused months are non-refundable on upfront packages.

What is the minimum campaign budget?+

For mobile geo-fencing, the entry point is $299/mo with 8,000 impressions per month. For CTV, impression packages start at $1,399 for 10,000 impressions — delivered at whatever pace works for your campaign.
