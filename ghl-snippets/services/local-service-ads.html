What Are Local Service Ads?

The most trusted lead source on Google — and you only pay when someone calls.

Local Service Ads (LSAs) are Google's pay-per-lead program for local businesses. Unlike PPC where you pay for every click, LSAs charge you only when a verified customer contacts you directly through Google. Your business earns the Google Verified badge — the blue checkmark that is the strongest trust signal Google offers in local search.

Google Verified BadgeThe blue checkmark that shows your business has passed Google's verification — background checks, licensing, and insurance.
High-Intent Inbound Calls OnlyLSA customers searched for your service, saw your badge, and chose to call you — not a cold prospect, an active buyer.
Budget That Goes to Leads — Not ClicksEvery dollar is accountable. Spam calls and job-type mismatches are disputable through Google's process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Google Local Service Ads

Still have questions? Call us at (866) 527-6205 — we're glad to walk through it.

With Google Ads you pay every time someone clicks your ad — even if they never call you. With LSA you only pay when a customer actually contacts you through Google. LSA ads also appear above paid search results, carry the Google Verified badge, and display your star rating right in the ad. For most service businesses, LSA delivers a significantly lower cost-per-lead. Plenty of clients run both — see our Google Ads management.

The $499 setup fee applies to the LSA Management plan and is collected at onboarding alongside your first month's payment — so your total day-one charge is $848. The setup fee is waived entirely on the Platinum Package. If you already have an active, verified LSA account in good standing, ask us on your strategy call — we review those on a case-by-case basis.

From kickoff call to live ads is typically 1–3 weeks. The main variable is Google's verification timeline — background checks and license reviews can take a few business days. We initiate everything in parallel so nothing waits on something else. Once verified, ads can go live within 24 hours of us activating the account.

Ad spend is set by you and billed directly by Google — completely separate from our management fee. Most clients start with a $500–$1,500/mo ad budget depending on their market and industry. We'll give you a specific recommendation on your strategy call. You control your budget and can adjust it anytime.

Google allows you to dispute leads that don't match your advertised services — spam calls, solicitations, wrong job types, repeat contacts, etc. On both plans, we actively review your lead history and submit disputes on your behalf. Approved disputes are refunded as credits to your Google account. Left unmanaged, a typical LSA account loses 15–25% of budget to undisputed bad leads.

The Platinum Package bundles LSA Management ($349/mo), Google Business Profile Management (normally $349/mo), and Reputation Management — all for $599/mo. More importantly, your LSA ads and your Google Business Profile work together: a well-optimized profile with strong reviews directly improves your LSA ranking, because Google's algorithm weighs your ratings and review volume when deciding who to show first.

The 3-, 6-, and 12-month tiers are paid in full upfront in exchange for the discount. There are no contracts or penalties beyond that — you simply pay the full period amount at the start. Unused months are non-refundable, but there's no additional fee or obligation. Month-to-month plans can cancel with 30 days' notice.
