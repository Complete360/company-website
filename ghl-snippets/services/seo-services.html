Why it matters now

Customers are asking AI who to hire.

Someone with a leaking roof doesn't always scroll through ten links anymore. They ask an AI assistant for a recommendation, or read the AI summary Google shows above the results.

Those answers come from websites. AI tools lean on sites that clearly say what a business does, where it works and why people trust it. If your site is thin, outdated or vague, the answer names someone else.

Most local business websites were never written with this in mind. That's the gap we close.

How we make your site AI-ready

  • Let AI tools inWe check that ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI aren't blocked from reading your site. Many sites block them without knowing it.
  • Answer real customer questionsEach service page answers what people ask before they hire, in plain sentences an AI tool can quote.
  • Label your business for machinesStructured data (behind-the-scenes code) spells out your services, service areas and business details.

No one controls what an AI tool says. What we can do is make your website the clearest, most trustworthy source for your services in your area, and check every month whether AI tools are mentioning you.
