How it works

A steady plan, not a one-time fix.

SEO compounds. Each month builds on the last, so the work never stops at a launch date.

  1. To start

    Audit and keyword plan

    We review your site, your competitors and your service area, then agree on the searches to target first.

  2. Next

    Fix and optimize

    Technical issues get fixed and your most important pages get rewritten and structured to rank.

  3. Every month

    Build new pages and content

    Two new service-area pages and two blog posts go live, each aimed at searches that bring in jobs.

  4. Every month

    Report and adjust

    You get your report and a strategy call. We double down on what's climbing and adjust what isn't.
