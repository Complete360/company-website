FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from dispensary owners before launching a geo-fencing campaign.

Is geo-fencing advertising legal for cannabis businesses?+

Yes. Geo-fencing runs through app networks and CTV publishers that allow cannabis advertising — unlike Google and Meta which have platform-level bans. All campaigns are designed to align with applicable state advertising regulations. We recommend verifying your state's rules before launch, and our team can help guide that conversation.

How does geo-fencing reach a 21+ audience without cookies or age verification?+

By targeting real-world locations where 21+ attendance is guaranteed — American Legion and VFW posts, dispensary proximity zones, and licensed event venues. Age is inferred by physical location, which is a legally accepted method in the geo-fencing industry and avoids data privacy issues tied to behavioral targeting.

What creative is included in my package?+

Our in-house design team builds all ad formats: mobile banners (300×250, 320×50, 728×90), CTV video creatives (15s or 30s), and desktop display. We work from your logo, brand colors, photos, and a quick brief. Nothing goes live without your explicit approval — no extra design fees.

Can I target a competitor's dispensary?+

Absolutely — this is one of the most powerful geo-fencing strategies. We draw a precise boundary around a competitor's location and serve your ads to anyone who walks in there. Completely legal, highly targeted, and one of the highest-ROI tactics available for local dispensary marketing.

What is the Veteran Outreach Starter package?+

The $1,399/year Veteran Outreach Starter targets the 5 closest American Legion and VFW posts to your shop at 1,000 impressions/mo. A portion of proceeds funds veteran resources through our Home Town Hero Partners initiative. It can be run standalone or stacked with a broader impressions package.

Can I run mobile and CTV in the same campaign?+

Yes. Our 200k+ packages include both mobile banner and CTV video placements. Mobile reaches your audience on their phones throughout the day. CTV hits them on the big screen during prime evening hours. Together they create a multi-touch presence that dramatically improves brand recall and conversion.

How long does it take to launch?+

Most campaigns go live within 5–7 business days of onboarding. That includes geo-fence setup, creative production, your approval review, and campaign activation. Emily manages the entire process — no need to coordinate with multiple vendors or platforms.
