Frequently asked questions

Everything You Need to Know Before Getting Started

How does local screen advertising work?

We place digital screens inside busy local businesses. Your ad joins a short rotation with a handful of other advertisers and plays full screen throughout the hours the venue is open.

Where will my ads appear?

On screens inside named local businesses near you: restaurants, salons, gyms, waiting rooms and shops. We'll tell you exactly which venues are available in your market before you commit.

What kinds of businesses can advertise with Complete360?

Most local businesses do well: home services, real estate, restaurants, health and wellness, auto, retail and more. We limit how many similar businesses share a screen.

Do you help create the ad?

Yes. Our team designs a screen-ready ad for you, or adapts artwork you already have. You review and approve it before anything goes live.

How quickly can a campaign launch?

Most campaigns go live within days. Once your ad is approved and your screen is confirmed, we schedule it to start right away.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your market, the number of screens and how long you run. Fill out the form and we'll send options that fit your budget.

What markets do you serve?

We have active placements across Arizona and can source screens in other markets on request. Tell us where you want to reach and we'll check availability.

How do I check availability?

Fill out the form below or give us a call. We'll check which screens and categories near you have open spots, free of charge.

Can I run seasonal or short-term promotions?

Yes. Short-term campaigns and ad refreshes for seasonal deals or new services are welcome. Send us the update and we'll swap your creative.

How do I get started?

Fill out the form below. We'll review your market, walk you through options on a quick call and help you pick the right next step, with no pressure and no obligation.
