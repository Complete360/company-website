Questions about marketing for your industry

What kind of marketing works best for a local business?

It depends on how fast your customers decide. When they need someone right away, like a plumber or a locksmith, Local Services Ads and answering every call matter most. When they compare before booking, your Google Business Profile and reviews lead. When they take weeks to choose, like with a mortgage or an insurance policy, Meta Ads and steady follow-up do the most work.

Can my business run Google Local Services Ads?

Many home service and trade businesses can, but it depends on your category and your state. Property and casualty insurance agents, for example, generally only qualify in California and Florida. Before your ads run, Google checks your license where required, your insurance and backgrounds so you earn the Google Verified badge. We confirm your eligibility before we recommend Local Services Ads.

Do you work with industries that aren't listed here?

Yes. These pages cover the industries we work with most, but we help many other local businesses too. Book a free strategy call or call (866) 527-6205, and we'll recommend a plan based on how your customers find you.

Do you only work with businesses in Arizona?

No. Complete360 has offices in Tucson and Phoenix, Arizona, and has served local businesses nationwide since 2018.

How much does marketing for my industry cost?

It depends on the services in your plan. Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, Google Business Profile Management and the AI Voice Agent. Google PPC and Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is separate and billed directly by Google or Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.

Can you guarantee more leads or a top ranking?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Google PPC, Meta Ads and Geo-Fencing, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.
