Section 1: Common questions

Auto shop marketing questions

How do auto repair shops get more customers?

Be at the top when drivers search, win them over with reviews, and stay top of mind for the next service. For most shops that starts with Local Services Ads and a Google Business Profile with steady new reviews. Weekly Google posts and Geo-Fencing around your shop come next.

Can auto repair shops use Google Local Services Ads?

In many areas, yes. Google offers Local Services Ads for car repair, so drivers can call your shop straight from the top of the search page. You pay per lead instead of per click, and the ad shows your rating and Google Verified badge. Availability varies by location, so we'll confirm eligibility for your area on the strategy call.

How do I compete with dealerships and big chains?

Don't try to outspend them; out-trust them. Strong recent reviews, real photos of your shop and team, and a profile that's active every week give drivers a reason to choose a local independent shop over a dealership service department.

How do I get more reviews from my customers?

Ask every customer, every time, right after the job. Google Business Profile Management includes review requests sent with a direct link, so leaving a review takes seconds, and responses to the reviews that come in.

What does Google Social Posting do for an auto shop?

It keeps your Google Business Profile active with posts every week: seasonal reminders like winter battery checks, specials and shop updates, written and designed for you. An active profile looks more trustworthy and gives drivers a reason to book.

How do I get customers to come back for routine maintenance?

Stay in front of them between visits. Weekly Google posts remind drivers about seasonal maintenance, and Geo-Fencing keeps your name on their phones and TVs around your area, so when it's time for the next oil change, they think of you.

Can you guarantee a number of new customers?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of customers. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads and Geo-Fencing, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.
