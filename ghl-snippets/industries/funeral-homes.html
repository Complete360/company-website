How families choose today

The funeral home reality

Families used to choose the funeral home their parents used. Today, many start with a search. Here's what they're looking for, and what that means for you.

What families are looking for

  • Someone they can reach right away

    When a loved one passes, families need a phone number, an address and the reassurance that someone will answer.

  • Proof of kindness from other families

    Reviews from families you've served speak more clearly than anything you can say about yourself.

  • Gentle, clear information

    Many want to understand their options and plan ahead without feeling pressured.

What funeral homes are facing

  • Loyalty isn't passed down like it was

    Families move, and younger generations often don't know which funeral home their family has used.

  • Larger groups and online providers advertise heavily

    Independent homes with generations of care can be harder to find than companies with bigger budgets.

  • Pre-planning is a hard conversation to start

    Many people intend to plan ahead and never take the first step without a gentle invitation.

Funeral home marketing questions

How do funeral homes reach more families today?

By being easy to find and clearly trusted when families search. That starts with an accurate Google Business Profile and reviews from families you've served. A website with clear, compassionate information and respectful pre-planning outreach come next.

Is it appropriate for a funeral home to advertise?

Yes, when it's done with care. We focus on helping families find accurate information and on gentle pre-planning invitations, never on pressure. Every ad and message is sent to you for approval before anything runs, so the tone always reflects your home.

Why do reviews matter so much for a funeral home?

Families who don't have a long relationship with a funeral home look to other families for reassurance. Google Business Profile Management includes asking families for reviews at an appropriate time and thoughtful responses to the reviews you receive.

How can we encourage more people to pre-plan?

Make the first step small and pressure-free. Meta Ads can offer something simple, like a free pre-planning guide, to people in your community. Your website gives them information at their own pace, and Complete360 CRM helps you follow up kindly with anyone who asks to hear more.

What should a funeral home website include?

The essentials families look for: how to reach you at any hour, where you are, the services you offer, your staff and your history, and information about planning ahead. Options and pricing for our websites are on our website pricing page.
