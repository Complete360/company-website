Why it's harder than it looks

You can build anything. Keeping a steady line of good projects coming in, without living on referrals and bid sites, is the part nobody trained you for.

01

Referrals are great until they stop

03

How do general contractors get more remodeling leads?

Make your company easy to find and easy to trust while homeowners are researching. Start with a strong Google Business Profile full of project photos and fresh reviews, plus Local Services Ads at the top of contractor searches. Then add Google PPC for remodel searches, Meta Ads with your before-and-afters, and a website that turns visits into estimate requests.

Can general contractors use Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. General contractor is one of the categories Google offers Local Services Ads for. You pay per lead rather than per click, and the ad shows your rating and Google Verified badge. Because remodels are researched over weeks, we run it alongside Google Business Profile Management rather than on its own. We'll confirm eligibility for your area on the strategy call.

Why does my Google Business Profile matter so much?

It's often the first thing a homeowner sees about your company, in the map results and when they search your name. Project photos, accurate service areas and recent reviews tell them you're established and active. Your reviews also affect how your Local Services Ads rank.

How do I attract bigger projects instead of small jobs?

Show the work you want more of and aim your ads at it. Google PPC can focus on searches like "kitchen remodel contractor" or "home addition builder," Meta Ads can feature your largest projects, and an estimate form that asks about scope and budget helps you spend your time on the right walkthroughs.

Do I need a new website as a general contractor?

Not always. If your current site shows your projects clearly, works well on a phone and makes it easy to request an estimate, we can work with it. If it doesn't, we build contractor websites with project galleries and estimate request forms. Options and pricing are on our website pricing page.

Do you guarantee a number of leads or projects?

No. No one can promise rankings, lead counts or signed projects. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Google PPC and Meta Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

What does it cost, and how do contracts work?

Google PPC and Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta.
