Real estate marketing questions

How do real estate agents get more leads online?

Reach buyers and sellers early, look like the local expert when they check you out, and follow up until they're ready. For most agents that starts with Meta Ads and a Google Business Profile with steady new reviews. A CRM for follow-up, Geo-Fencing around open houses and farm areas, and a website of your own come next.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for real estate agents?

They're one of the best ways to reach people months before they're ready to buy or sell, with offers like new-listing alerts and home value reviews. Meta treats real estate ads as housing ads, which limits how they can be targeted, and fair housing rules apply too. We build campaigns within those rules and focus on the offer and the follow-up.

Why does a Google Business Profile matter for an agent?

When a lead searches your name, your Google Business Profile is often the first thing they see. Recent reviews, photos and posts about your sales and your market show them you're active and trusted, which matters most when they're choosing between agents.

How do I follow up with leads who aren't ready yet?

Put them on a schedule. Complete360 CRM keeps every lead and past client in one pipeline and sends automatic texts and emails, like new listings, check-ins and market updates, so you stay top of mind without having to remember who to call.

Can I advertise around my open houses or farm area?

Yes. Geo-Fencing shows your ads on phones and connected TVs in the places you choose, like the neighborhood around an open house or the area you farm. Housing ads still have to follow fair housing rules and each platform's policies, so campaigns are built around places and offers, not around who people are.

Do I need my own website if my brokerage gives me one?

A brokerage page works, but it usually isn't yours to keep or customize. Your own website holds your listings, your story and your lead forms, and it stays with you if you change brokerages. Options and pricing are on our website pricing page.

Can you guarantee a number of leads or closings?

No. No one can promise lead counts or closings. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Meta Ads and Geo-Fencing, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is billed directly by Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
