Garage door marketing questions

How do garage door companies get more calls?

Show up at the top of Google when a door breaks, answer every call, and keep fresh reviews coming. For most companies that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management) and an AI Voice Agent. Google PPC, the Local SEO Booster and Meta Ads come next.

Can garage door companies run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes, but Google holds garage door services to a higher bar. Your business has to pass Google's Advanced Verification before ads can run, and Google doesn't offer early pre-badge ads for this category. Expect license (where your state requires one), insurance and background checks. We walk you through each step so your ads can go live with the Google Verified badge.

Why is it so hard to show up on Google Maps for garage door repair?

It's one of the most crowded categories on Google. A verified profile, consistent business details across the web, steady new reviews and regular updates give Google reasons to trust that you're a real, local company. That's what Google Business Profile Management and the Local SEO Booster are for.

What happens to calls when my techs are on jobs?

The AI Voice Agent answers them, day or night. It takes the homeowner's name, address and what's wrong, and books the visit or passes the details to your team. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

How do I sell more new doors and openers, not just repairs?

Reach people while they're comparing. Google PPC puts you in front of searches for new door installation, openers and commercial doors, and Meta Ads show before-and-after photos to homeowners in the neighborhoods you serve.

Are Local Services Ads leads shared with other companies?

No. Homeowners call or message your business directly from your ad, and you pay for leads rather than clicks. That's why answering every call quickly matters so much.

Can you guarantee more jobs?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or jobs. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Google PPC and Meta Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does garage door marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, the AI Voice Agent and the Local SEO Booster. Google PPC and Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Google or Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
