Handyman marketing questions

How do handyman businesses get more jobs?

Show up at the top of Google with proof you're trusted, and make it clear what you can fix. For most handyman businesses that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management, reviews included). Meta Ads, Google Social Posting and a website that lists your services come next.

Can handymen run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Handyman is an eligible Local Services Ads category. Google checks your business license where your state requires one, your insurance and backgrounds before your ads earn the Google Verified badge. We walk you through each step.

How do I get bigger jobs instead of just small fixes?

Show people what else you do. Weekly Google posts and before-and-after ads put your bigger projects in front of past customers and their neighbors, and Google PPC can reach people searching for the projects you want more of.

How do I compete with gig apps and lowball quotes?

Look like the safe choice. The Google Verified badge, steady reviews that mention you by name, real photos of your work and a clear list of services tell homeowners they're hiring a real local business, not a stranger from an app.

What happens to calls while I'm on a job?

The AI Voice Agent can answer them. It takes the caller's name, address and list of jobs, and books the visit or passes the details to you. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for handymen?

Yes. Before-and-after photos and seasonal honey-do offers shown to homeowners nearby remind them about the list they've been putting off. Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is billed directly by Meta.

Can you guarantee more jobs?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or jobs. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Meta Ads and Google PPC, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does handyman marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, Google Social Posting and websites. Meta Ads and Google PPC are planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Meta or Google. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
