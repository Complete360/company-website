Landscaping marketing questions

How do landscaping and hardscaping companies get more projects?

Show up when homeowners start planning, show off your finished work, and follow up while they decide. For most design-build companies that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management). Meta Ads, Google PPC and a project-gallery website come next.

Can landscaping companies run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Landscaping is an eligible Local Services Ads category. Google checks your business license where your state requires one, your insurance and backgrounds before your ads earn the Google Verified badge. We walk you through each step.

How do I get more hardscape projects instead of mowing calls?

Aim every channel at the work you want. In Local Services Ads you choose the job types you offer, Google PPC can target searches like patio installers and retaining walls, and your profile, website and ads should lead with finished projects. If maintenance is most of your business, our lawn care marketing page is a better fit.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for landscapers?

Yes. Outdoor projects are some of the most visual work there is, and finished patios and yards shown to homeowners nearby get people planning. Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is billed directly by Meta.

How do I win more of the designs I propose?

Stay in touch while they decide. The Complete360 CRM keeps every proposal in one place with friendly follow-ups, and strong reviews and project photos make your company the easy choice when they compare.

How do I keep busy in fall and winter?

Book next spring now. Ads that invite homeowners to plan early, and a steady stream of design consultations through the quiet months, put installs on your calendar before the spring rush starts.

Can you guarantee more projects?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or projects. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Meta Ads, Google PPC and Geo-Fencing, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does landscaping marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, websites and the Complete360 CRM. Meta Ads and Google PPC are planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Meta or Google. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
