Moving company marketing questions

How do moving companies get more booked moves?

Be the mover customers trust when they search, respond fast, and follow up on every estimate. For most moving companies that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management). Google PPC, Meta Ads and the Complete360 CRM for estimate follow-up come next, with an AI Voice Agent available when you're ready.

Can moving companies run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Moving services is a Local Services Ads category, though availability can vary by area. Google checks your business license or registration where required, your insurance and backgrounds before your ads earn the Google Verified badge. We walk you through each step.

Are Local Services Ads leads shared like lead-site leads?

No. Customers call or message your business directly from your ad, and you pay for leads rather than clicks. The lead isn't sold to a list of other movers at the same time.

How do I turn more estimates into booked moves?

Follow up quickly and consistently. Most customers compare several quotes, and the mover who checks in, answers questions and makes it easy to hold a date often wins. The Complete360 CRM keeps every estimate in one place with friendly follow-up texts, and strong reviews make the decision easier.

How do I fill the slow weeks of the month?

Use marketing you can aim at the quiet days. Google PPC can focus on long-distance, commercial and specialty moves that book further out, and Meta Ads can promote offers for mid-month moves to people in your service area.

What happens to calls when every crew is out?

The AI Voice Agent can answer them. It takes the caller's name, move date, where they're moving from and to and the size of the home, and books the estimate or passes the details to your team. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

Can you guarantee more moves?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or moves. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Google PPC and Meta Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does moving company marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, the Complete360 CRM and the AI Voice Agent. Google PPC and Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Google or Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
