Electrician marketing questions

How do electricians get more calls?

Show up first with proof you're licensed and trusted, and answer every call, day or night. For most electrical companies that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management) and an AI Voice Agent. Google PPC for panel upgrades and EV chargers, and the Local SEO Booster, come next.

Can electricians run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Electricians are one of Google's core Local Services Ads categories. Google checks your electrical license where your state requires one, your insurance and backgrounds before your ads earn the Google Verified badge. We walk you through each step.

What happens to emergency calls after hours?

The AI Voice Agent answers them. It takes the caller's name, address and what's happening, and sends urgent calls to your on-call electrician or books the visit for the morning. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

How do I get more panel upgrades and EV charger installs?

Reach people while they're planning. Google PPC puts you in front of searches for panel upgrades, EV charger installation and generators, and a CRM keeps your estimate in front of them with friendly follow-ups while they compare bids.

How do I show homeowners we're licensed and safe?

Make the proof easy to find. The Google Verified badge, steady reviews that mention careful, tidy work, real photos of your team and trucks, and your license details on your profile and website all reassure people before they call.

Are Local Services Ads leads shared with other electricians?

No. Homeowners call or message your business directly from your ad, and you pay for leads rather than clicks. That's why answering every call quickly matters so much.

Can you guarantee more jobs?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or jobs. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Google PPC and Meta Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does electrician marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, the AI Voice Agent and the Local SEO Booster. Google PPC and Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Google or Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
