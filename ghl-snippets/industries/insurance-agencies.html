Insurance agency marketing questions

Straight answers for P&C agents. Ask us anything else on a strategy call.

Ask us on a call
How do P&C insurance agents get more leads?

Be easy to find, look trustworthy when people check you out, and respond fast. For most agencies that starts with a Google Business Profile with steady new reviews and Meta Ads with permission-based lead forms. A CRM for renewals, Geo-Fencing and an AI Voice Agent for missed calls come next.

Can my insurance agency run Google Local Services Ads?

Only if you're licensed in California or Florida. Those are the only states where Google currently offers Local Services Ads to insurance agencies. Google checks each agent's state license and background before your ads earn the Google Verified badge. In every other state, we lead with Meta Ads and your Google Business Profile.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for insurance agents?

Yes, when they're set up the right way. Meta requires insurance ads to run in its financial products and services category, which limits targeting by age, gender and detailed location, so we build campaigns around a clear offer and the area you serve. Lead forms ask for permission to contact the person, and those leads come to you, not to a shared list.

I'm a captive agent. Can you still help?

Yes. Many carriers have rules for their agents' ads and websites, and some need to approve them first. Tell us your carrier's requirements and we'll work within them. You approve every ad, page and form before anything goes live.

How do I keep clients from leaving at renewal?

Reach out before the renewal notice does. The Complete360 CRM sends renewal reminders and coverage check-ins ahead of each renewal date to clients who've given you permission to contact them, so you can talk about a rate change before a competitor does.

Will the AI Voice Agent quote rates to callers?

No. It answers calls you miss, takes the caller's name, number and what they're looking for, and books a time with a licensed agent. It doesn't quote, compare or bind coverage. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

Can you guarantee more policies?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or policies. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Meta Ads, Geo-Fencing and Local Services Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does insurance agency marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including Google Business Profile Management, the Google Platinum Package, Geo-Fencing, the Complete360 CRM and the AI Voice Agent. Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by the ad platform. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.

Complete360 is a marketing agency for licensed insurance agents and agencies. We don't sell insurance and aren't affiliated with any insurance carrier or government agency.
