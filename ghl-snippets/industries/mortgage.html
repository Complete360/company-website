Mortgage marketing questions

How do loan officers get more mortgage leads?

Reach homebuyers before they've picked a lender, look trustworthy when they and their agents check you out, and stay in touch until they're ready. For most loan officers that starts with Meta Ads and a Google Business Profile with steady new reviews. A CRM for follow-up and an AI Voice Agent for missed calls come next.

Do Facebook ads work for mortgage loan officers?

They're a strong way to reach people house-hunting in your market, months before they apply. Meta treats mortgage ads as a special ad category for financial products, which limits how they can be targeted, so campaigns focus on the offer and the follow-up rather than narrow audiences.

How do you handle mortgage advertising rules?

We write ads without rate, payment or approval promises, include your NMLS information, and send every ad, landing page and form to you and your compliance team for approval before it runs. As the licensed loan officer, you and your company have final say on compliance.

How do I get more referrals from real estate agents?

Agents refer lenders who make their clients happy and close on time. Reviews from borrowers and agent partners on your Google Business Profile show that, and regular check-ins through your CRM keep you top of mind with the agents you already work with.

How do I stay in touch with past clients?

Put them on a schedule. Complete360 CRM sends home anniversary messages and annual review reminders to past borrowers who've given you permission to contact them, so you're the first call when they move or refinance.

Can an AI Voice Agent answer calls for a loan officer?

Yes, for scheduling. It answers inbound calls, takes the caller's details and schedules a call with you. It doesn't quote rates, take applications or give financial advice; that conversation is yours. AI usage is unlimited with no per-minute AI fees. Your phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

Can you guarantee a number of leads or closed loans?

No. No one can promise lead counts or closings. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Meta Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does mortgage marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including Google Business Profile Management, Complete360 CRM and the AI Voice Agent. Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is billed directly by Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
