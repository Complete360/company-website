Junk removal marketing questions

How do junk removal companies get more jobs?

Show up at the top of Google with proof you're trusted, and answer every call. For most junk removal companies that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management) and an AI Voice Agent. Meta Ads and Google Social Posting come next.

Can junk removal companies run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Junk removal is an eligible Local Services Ads category. Google checks your business license where your state requires one, your insurance and backgrounds before your ads earn the Google Verified badge. We walk you through each step.

What happens to calls while my crew is on a job?

The AI Voice Agent answers them. It takes the caller's name and address, asks what needs to go and where it is, and books the pickup or passes the details to your team. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

How do I compete with the big junk removal franchises?

Win locally. The Google Verified badge, steady reviews that mention your crew by name, real photos of your trucks and jobs, and a phone that's always answered are what homeowners compare, and a local company can beat a national brand on every one.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for junk removal?

Yes. Before-and-after cleanout photos are some of the most eye-catching ads a local business can run. Seasonal offers for spring cleaning, moves and estate cleanouts, shown to neighbors nearby, keep your trucks busy on weekdays too.

Are Local Services Ads leads shared with other companies?

No. Customers call or message your business directly from your ad, and you pay for leads rather than clicks. That's why answering every call quickly matters so much.

Can you guarantee more jobs?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or jobs. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Meta Ads and Google PPC, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does junk removal marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, the AI Voice Agent and Google Social Posting. Meta Ads and Google PPC are planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Meta or Google. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
