Appliance repair marketing questions

How do appliance repair companies get more calls?

Show up at the top of Google with proof you're trusted, and answer every call. For most appliance repair companies that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management) and an AI Voice Agent. Google PPC and Google Social Posting come next.

Can appliance repair companies run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Appliance repair is an eligible Local Services Ads category. Google checks your business license where your state requires one, your insurance and backgrounds before your ads earn the Google Verified badge. We walk you through each step.

What happens to calls while my techs are on jobs?

The AI Voice Agent answers them. It takes the caller's name and address, the appliance, the brand and model number and what's wrong, and books the visit, so your tech can show up with the right part. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

How do I compete with big-box stores and manufacturer service?

Be the local company that's easy to reach and easy to trust. The Google Verified badge, a full profile with real photos, steady reviews that mention fast, honest repairs, and a phone that's always answered are things a big store's service line can't match.

How do I fill the slow weeks?

Use marketing you can turn up when there's room. Google PPC can focus on the repairs you want more of during quiet stretches, and weekly Google posts with tips and specials keep your profile active between busy seasons.

Are Local Services Ads leads shared with other companies?

No. Homeowners call or message your business directly from your ad, and you pay for leads rather than clicks. That's why answering every call quickly matters so much.

Can you guarantee more jobs?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or jobs. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads and Google PPC, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does appliance repair marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, the AI Voice Agent and Google Social Posting. Google PPC is planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Google. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
