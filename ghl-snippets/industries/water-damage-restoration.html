Restoration marketing questions

How do water damage restoration companies get more jobs?

Show up first with proof you're trusted, and answer every emergency call, day or night. For most restoration companies that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management) and an AI Voice Agent. Google PPC for mold, flood and rebuild work and the Local SEO Booster come next.

Can restoration companies run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Water damage services is an eligible Local Services Ads category. Google checks your business license where your state requires one, your insurance and backgrounds before your ads earn the Google Verified badge. We walk you through each step.

What happens to emergency calls in the middle of the night?

The AI Voice Agent answers them. It takes the caller's name, address, the water source and whether it's still running, and sends it to your on-call crew so you can call back right away. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

Do I still need marketing if insurers and plumbers send me work?

Referral work is valuable, but it can slow down or change hands without warning. Homeowners who find you on Google give you jobs you don't depend on anyone else for, and a CRM helps you stay in touch with the plumbers, property managers and agents who do refer you.

How do I get more mold remediation and rebuild work?

Those customers usually research before they call. Google PPC puts you in front of searches for mold remediation, flood cleanup and repairs, and a Google Business Profile that lists those services helps you show up for them on the map.

Are Local Services Ads leads shared with other companies?

No. Homeowners call or message your business directly from your ad, and you pay for leads rather than clicks. That's why answering every call quickly matters so much.

Can you guarantee more jobs?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or jobs. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Google PPC and Geo-Fencing, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does restoration marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, the AI Voice Agent and the Local SEO Booster. Google PPC is planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by the ad platform. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
