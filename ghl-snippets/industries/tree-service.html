Tree service marketing questions

Getting more jobs

How do tree service companies get more jobs?

Show up at the top of Google with proof you're insured and trusted, and keep reviews coming. For most tree companies that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management). Google PPC for emergency removals, Meta Ads for seasonal trimming and Geo-Fencing for storm-hit neighborhoods come next.

Can tree service companies run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Tree services is an eligible Local Services Ads category. Google checks your business license where your state requires one, your insurance and backgrounds before your ads earn the Google Verified badge. We walk you through each step.

How do I handle storm calls at night?

Answer them, even when your crew is off the clock. The AI Voice Agent picks up day or night, takes the caller's name, address and what happened, and books the estimate or passes it to your team. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

How do I win more bids against cheaper quotes?

Show homeowners what they're paying for. The Google Verified badge, steady reviews that mention careful, clean work, and real photos of your crew and equipment make your quote feel like the safe choice, not the expensive one.

How do I fill the slow winter months?

Book planned work before you need it. Search ads for removals and stump grinding, and Meta Ads promoting winter pruning or early spring trimming, put jobs on the calendar while the phone would otherwise be quiet.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for tree services?

Yes, especially for planned work. Before-and-after photos and seasonal offers shown to homeowners nearby remind them about the overgrown tree they've been meaning to deal with. Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is billed directly by Meta.

Costs and commitments

Can you guarantee more jobs?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or jobs. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Google PPC, Meta Ads and Geo-Fencing, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does tree service marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, Geo-Fencing and the AI Voice Agent. Google PPC and Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Google or Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
