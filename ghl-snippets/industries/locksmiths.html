Locksmith marketing questions

How do locksmiths get more calls?

Show up at the top of Google with proof you're real and trusted, and answer every call, day or night. For most locksmiths that starts with the Google Platinum Package (Local Services Ads plus Google Business Profile Management) and an AI Voice Agent. Google PPC for rekeys and commercial work and the Local SEO Booster come next.

Can locksmiths run Google Local Services Ads?

Yes, but Google holds locksmiths to a higher bar. Your business has to pass Google's Advanced Verification before ads can run, and Google doesn't offer early pre-badge ads for locksmiths. Expect license (where your state requires one), insurance and background checks. We walk you through each step so your ads can go live with the Google Verified badge.

How do I stand out from fake locksmith listings?

Give people proof. The Google Verified badge, a complete profile with real photos of your team and vans, steady new reviews with replies, and consistent business details across the web all show you're a real, local company. That's what the Google Platinum Package and the Local SEO Booster are built to do.

What happens to lockout calls at night or when I'm on a job?

The AI Voice Agent answers them. It takes the caller's name, location and situation, and sends it to your on-call tech or books the appointment, so the job doesn't go to the next locksmith on the list. There are no per-minute AI fees; the phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

How do I get more rekeying and commercial work?

Those customers search differently than someone locked out. Google PPC puts you in front of searches like rekeying after a move, commercial locks and access control, and a Google Business Profile that lists those services helps you show up for them on the map.

Are Local Services Ads leads shared with other locksmiths?

No. Customers call or message your business directly from your ad, and you pay for leads rather than clicks. That's why answering every call quickly matters so much.

Can you guarantee more jobs?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads or jobs. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Google PPC and Meta Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does locksmith marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including the Google Platinum Package, the AI Voice Agent and the Local SEO Booster. Google PPC and Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call. Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Google or Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
