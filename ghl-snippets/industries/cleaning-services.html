The cleaning business reality

Cleaning is built on trust and repeat visits. These are the things that keep good cleaning companies from growing.

  • Homeowners are nervous about letting strangers in

    Before anyone gets a key or a door code, homeowners want proof you're safe, insured and reliable. Without it, they keep scrolling.

  • One-time deep cleans that never come back

    A move-out clean pays once. Recurring weekly and biweekly clients are what make the schedule and the revenue predictable.

  • Calls come in while your team is cleaning

    Hands are full and phones are in pockets. Every missed call is a homeowner booking with whoever picks up first.

  • Booking apps make you look like everyone else

    On a list of cheap options, cleaning becomes a price contest. Your own reviews and reputation are what set you apart.

Cleaning business marketing questions

How do cleaning companies get more clients?

Show up when homeowners search, prove you can be trusted in their home, and answer every inquiry quickly. For most cleaning companies that starts with Local Services Ads and a Google Business Profile with steady new reviews. Meta Ads for recurring and seasonal cleans and an AI Voice Agent for calls you can't take come next.

Can cleaning services use Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Cleaning services is one of the categories Google offers Local Services Ads for. You pay per lead instead of per click, and the ad shows your rating and Google Verified badge. Lead volume depends on your reviews, budget, service area and how quickly you respond, so we pair it with Google Business Profile Management. We'll confirm eligibility for your area on the strategy call.

What does Google Verified mean for a cleaning company?

It's the badge on Local Services Ads showing that Google has checked your business, which can include insurance and background checks depending on your state. For a service that works inside people's homes, that badge does a lot of the trust-building for you. It replaced the Google Guaranteed and Google Screened badges in October 2025, and we walk you through verification during setup.

How do I turn one-time cleans into recurring clients?

Make the first clean the start of a relationship. Ask every client for a review, keep your Google Business Profile current, and promote recurring service to homeowners nearby with Meta Ads. Homeowners who see your reviews and your name regularly are much more comfortable committing to weekly or biweekly service.

How can I answer calls while my team is cleaning?

The AI Voice Agent answers 24/7, asks about the home and the service the caller needs, captures their details, and books the estimate or routes the call to you, based on how you want calls handled. AI usage is unlimited with no per-minute AI fees. Your phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

How do I fill slow weeks on the schedule?

Promote the cleans people plan around: move-in and move-out cleans, spring deep cleans and pre-holiday cleans. Meta Ads can put those offers in front of homeowners in your service area right when they're thinking about them.

Can you guarantee a number of new clients?

No. No one can promise rankings, lead counts or bookings. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads and Meta Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does cleaning business marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including Local Service Ads, Google Business Profile Management and the AI Voice Agent. Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is billed directly by Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
