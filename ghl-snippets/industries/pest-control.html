Pest control marketing questions

Getting more customers

How do pest control companies get more customers?

Be at the top when homeowners search, look trustworthy when they compare, and answer every call. For most pest control companies that means Local Services Ads, a Google Business Profile with steady new reviews, and an AI Voice Agent for after-hours and overflow calls. Meta Ads that sell recurring protection plans come next.

Do Local Services Ads work for pest control?

For most residential pest control companies, yes. Pest control is one of the categories Google offers Local Services Ads for. You pay per lead instead of per click, and the ad shows your rating and Google Verified badge at the top of the page. Lead volume depends on your reviews, budget, service area and how quickly you answer, so we pair it with Google Business Profile Management. We'll confirm eligibility for your area on the strategy call.

What does Google Verified mean for a pest control company?

It's the badge on Local Services Ads showing that Google has checked your business, which can include your pest control license, insurance and background checks depending on your state. It replaced the Google Guaranteed and Google Screened badges in October 2025, and we walk you through verification during setup.

Can an AI Voice Agent answer after-hours pest control calls?

Yes. It answers 24/7, asks what the homeowner is seeing and where, captures their details, and books the treatment or routes urgent calls to you, based on how you want calls handled. AI usage is unlimited with no per-minute AI fees. Your phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

How do I sell more quarterly and monthly protection plans?

Ask every one-time customer, and remind the neighborhood regularly. Reviews that mention ongoing service build confidence, and Meta Ads can promote protection plans and seasonal prevention to homeowners in your area ahead of each pest season.

How should I market around pest seasons?

Be ready before each wave. Keep your Local Services Ads budget ready for the busy months, post seasonal updates on your Google Business Profile, and run Meta Ads for prevention ahead of ant, wasp or rodent season in your area.
