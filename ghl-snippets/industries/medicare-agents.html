Medicare agent marketing questions

How do Medicare agents get more clients?

Reach people before they turn 65, look like the trusted local agent when they check you out, and stay in touch every year. For most agents that starts with Meta Ads and a Google Business Profile with steady new reviews. An AI Voice Agent for busy-season calls and a CRM for annual follow-up come next.

Do Facebook ads work for Medicare agents?

They're one of the best ways to reach people approaching Medicare age in your area, months before they decide. We use lead forms that ask for permission to contact them, so the leads come to you directly instead of being shared with other agents. Every ad and form goes to you for approval before it runs.

How do you handle CMS marketing rules?

We write ads and pages that avoid plan or benefit claims, and every ad, landing page and form is sent to you for approval before it runs, so you can review it with your upline or carriers. As the licensed agent, you have final say on compliance.

Can an AI Voice Agent answer calls for a Medicare agency?

Yes, for scheduling. It answers inbound calls, takes the caller's details and puts an appointment on your calendar. It doesn't discuss, compare or enroll anyone in plans; that conversation is yours. AI usage is unlimited with no per-minute AI fees. Your phone number and calling minutes are billed separately. Confirm any call recording requirements with your compliance team.

How do I stay busy outside of the Annual Enrollment Period?

Focus on people turning 65, who need help every month of the year, and on your existing clients. Meta Ads keep turning-65 leads coming year-round, and annual review reminders through your CRM keep current clients coming back.

Why does a Google Business Profile matter for an independent agent?

When someone gets your name from an ad or a friend, they look you up. A Google Business Profile with your office, hours and reviews shows you're a real local agent, which matters to people who are wary of call centers.

Can you guarantee a number of leads or enrollments?

No. No one can promise lead counts or enrollments. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Meta Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

How much does Medicare agent marketing cost?

Each service's price is on its own page, including Google Business Profile Management, the AI Voice Agent and Complete360 CRM. Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is billed directly by Meta. To cancel a recurring service, give at least 10 days' written notice before the next billing date. Payments are non-refundable from the time of purchase.
