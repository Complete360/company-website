The plumbing reality

Most plumbing jobs start with a problem that can't wait. The company that's easiest to find, trust and reach gets the call, and often every call after that.

  1. 01

    The emergencies come at the worst times

    A burst pipe at 2 a.m., a backed-up drain on Thanksgiving morning. If nobody picks up, the homeowner calls the next plumber on the list, and that plumber gets the repeat business too.

  2. 02

    Water on the floor means no time to shop around

    Homeowners in a panic don't read five websites. They call whoever is at the top of Google with good reviews and a phone number they can tap.

  3. 03

    Small service calls don't pay for the trucks

    Clogs and running toilets keep the phones busy, but water heaters, repipes and sewer lines are what make the month. Those homeowners search differently and take longer to decide.

  4. 04

    Wrong info online sends calls elsewhere

    An old phone number on Yelp, the wrong hours on Apple Maps, a duplicate listing on Bing. Every mismatch costs calls you never knew you missed.

Plumbing marketing questions

What's the best way for a plumber to get more calls?

Be at the top of Google when someone has a plumbing emergency, have the reviews to earn their trust, and answer every call. For most residential plumbers, that means Local Services Ads, Google Business Profile Management with steady review requests, and an AI Voice Agent for after-hours and overflow calls. Google PPC for bigger jobs and the Local SEO Booster for accurate listings come next.

Do Local Services Ads work for plumbers?

For most residential plumbing companies, they do. Plumbing is one of the categories Google offers Local Services Ads for, you're charged per lead instead of per click, and the ad shows your reviews and Google Verified badge at the top of the page. Results depend on your reviews, budget, service area and how quickly you answer, which is why we pair them with Google Business Profile Management. What is Google Verified for plumbers?

Google Verified is the badge on Local Services Ads that tells homeowners Google has checked your business, which can include your plumbing license, insurance and background checks depending on your state. It replaced the Google Guaranteed and Google Screened badges in October 2025. We help you through the verification process during setup.

Can an AI Voice Agent handle emergency plumbing calls?

Yes. It answers 24/7, asks what the problem is and where, captures the caller's details, and either books the job or routes urgent calls to your on-call plumber, based on how you want calls handled. AI usage is unlimited with no per-minute AI fees. Your phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

How do I get more water heater, repipe and sewer line jobs?

Reach homeowners while they're researching the job, before they've picked a plumber. Google PPC puts your company in front of searches like "tankless water heater installation" or "sewer line replacement," and recent reviews that mention those jobs help homeowners trust you with the bigger ticket.
