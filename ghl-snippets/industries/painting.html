Painting marketing questions

How do painting contractors get more leads?

Make it easy for homeowners to find you and easy to see the quality of your work. Start with Local Services Ads at the top of painter searches and a Google Business Profile full of before-and-after photos and fresh reviews. Then add Meta Ads to show your transformations around town and Google PPC for exterior and whole-house projects.

Can painters use Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. Painting is one of the categories Google offers Local Services Ads for. You pay per lead instead of per click, and the ad shows your rating and Google Verified badge. Lead volume depends on your reviews, budget, service area and how quickly you respond, so we pair it with Google Business Profile Management. We'll confirm eligibility for your area on the strategy call.

What does Google Verified mean for a painting company?

It's the badge on Local Services Ads showing that Google has checked your business, which can include your contractor license, insurance and background checks depending on your state. It replaced the Google Guaranteed and Google Screened badges in October 2025, and we walk you through verification during setup.

How do I compete with painters who bid lower?

Give homeowners a reason to pay for quality. A steady stream of recent reviews, photos that show your prep and finish work, and ads that keep your name in front of them all make the cheapest bid look like the risky one. Homeowners who already trust you before the estimate are less likely to shop on price alone.

How do I book exterior jobs before the season starts?

Reach homeowners while they're still planning. Meta Ads with your exterior before-and-afters can run in the weeks before your season, and Google PPC catches people searching for exterior painting quotes. Booking early helps you plan crews instead of scrambling once the weather turns.

Why do before-and-after photos matter so much?

Painting is judged by how it looks, and homeowners can't picture your quality from a description. Before-and-after photos on your Google Business Profile and in your ads show exactly what they'll get, which builds trust faster than anything you could write.

Can you guarantee a number of painting leads?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of leads. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, Meta Ads and Google PPC, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

Meta Ads and Google PPC are planned with you on a strategy call, and ad spend is billed directly by Meta or Google.
