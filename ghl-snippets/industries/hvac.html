The HVAC reality

HVAC demand follows the thermometer, not your marketing calendar. The companies that grow plan for both extremes.

Every phone rings on the same afternoon

The first heat wave or cold snap brings a month of calls in a week. Your techs are booked, the office is buried, and the overflow goes to whoever answers next.

Homeowners call three names and book the first to pick up

A family with no AC isn't comparing websites. They tap the top results, check the stars, and hire the company that answers.

Spring and fall go quiet

Between seasons the emergency calls dry up, but payroll and truck payments don't. Tune-ups and maintenance plans are what keep crews busy.

Shared leads mean racing four other shops

Bought leads often go to several contractors at once. You end up competing on speed and price for a job that was never really yours.

HVAC marketing questions

How do HVAC companies get more calls from Google?

Show up where homeowners look when their system fails, look trustworthy when they compare, and answer every call. In practice that means Local Services Ads at the top of the search, a well-kept Google Business Profile in the map results, a steady flow of fresh reviews, and someone (or an AI Voice Agent) picking up nights and weekends. Google PPC and Meta Ads come next, once those basics are working.

Are Local Services Ads worth it for HVAC companies?

For most residential HVAC companies, yes. HVAC is one of the categories Google offers Local Services Ads for, you pay per lead instead of per click, and the ad shows your rating and Google Verified badge above the regular search ads. How many leads you get depends on your reviews, your service area, your budget and how quickly you answer, which is why we pair it with Google Business Profile and review management. We confirm eligibility for your area on the strategy call.

What does Google Verified mean for an HVAC company?

Google Verified is the badge Google shows on Local Services Ads once it has checked your business, such as your license, insurance and background checks, depending on your state. It replaced the older Google Guaranteed and Google Screened badges in October 2025. We walk you through the verification steps during setup.

How do I get more HVAC calls in spring and fall?

Sell what homeowners plan ahead for: tune-ups, maintenance plans and replacement estimates. Meta Ads put those offers in front of homeowners in your service area before the season turns, and Google PPC reaches people researching a new system. Maintenance plan members also give your crews scheduled work when emergency calls slow down.

Can an AI Voice Agent answer after-hours HVAC calls?

Yes. The AI Voice Agent answers 24/7, asks about the problem, captures the caller's details, and books the appointment or routes urgent calls to you. AI usage is unlimited with no per-minute AI fees. Your phone number and calling minutes are billed separately.

No. No one can promise Google rankings or a set number of leads, and we won't. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.
