Restaurant marketing questions

How do restaurants get more customers from Google?

Show up on the map with accurate hours, great photos and plenty of recent reviews, and keep your profile active. For most restaurants that starts with Google Business Profile Management and weekly Google posts. Meta Ads for specials and events, Geo-Fencing and the Local SEO Booster come next.

Why does my Google Business Profile matter so much?

It's often the first and only thing a hungry diner looks at. Your hours, photos, menu link, price range and reviews all appear right on the map, and diners decide in seconds whether to go or keep scrolling.

How should a restaurant handle negative reviews?

Reply calmly and kindly, and keep fresh positive reviews coming. Google Business Profile Management includes review requests to your guests and thoughtful responses to the reviews you receive, so one bad night doesn't define your restaurant.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for restaurants?

They're one of the best ways to show off your food to people nearby. Ads for weeknight specials, happy hour, events and new menu items give locals a reason to come in this week instead of someday.

How do I fill tables on slow nights?

Give people a specific reason to come in on that night, and tell them at the right time. Weekly Google posts and Meta Ads can promote a Tuesday special or a Wednesday event in the days leading up to it.

How do I keep my hours right on every app?

The Local SEO Booster keeps your name, address, phone number and hours accurate across 70+ directories, maps and AI assistants, including Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing, so a holiday schedule change doesn't send diners to a locked door.

Can you guarantee more diners?

No. No one can promise rankings or a set number of guests. For Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Meta Ads and Geo-Fencing, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. That applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.
