The roofing reality

Roofing runs on weather and trust. One hailstorm can fill a month, and one bad experience with a door knocker makes every homeowner on the street cautious.

Storms bring a flood of calls, then silence

After hail or high winds, everyone needs an inspection at once. Weeks later the phone goes quiet, and the crew still needs work.

Out-of-town crews knock first

Roofing marketing questions

How do roofing companies get more leads from Google?

Be at the top when homeowners search, look established when they check you out, and follow up while they decide. For most roofers that starts with Local Services Ads and a well-managed Google Business Profile with steady new reviews. Google PPC for replacement searches, Geo-Fencing in the neighborhoods you work, and Meta Ads with project photos come next.

Are Local Services Ads worth it for roofers?

For most residential roofing companies, yes. Roofing is one of the categories Google offers Local Services Ads for. You pay for leads rather than clicks, and your ad shows your star rating and Google Verified badge at the top of the search page. Lead volume depends on your reviews, budget, service area and how fast you respond, so we run it alongside Google Business Profile Management. We'll confirm eligibility for your area on the strategy call.

What does Google Verified mean for a roofing company?

It's the badge Google puts on Local Services Ads after checking your business. Depending on your state, that can include your contractor license, insurance and background checks. Google Verified took the place of the Google Guaranteed and Google Screened badges in October 2025, and we guide you through each step of verification.

How should I market my roofing company after a storm?

Make sure homeowners can find you, trust you and reach you right away. Keep your Local Services Ads budget ready for the surge, post storm updates and recent project photos on your Google Business Profile, and answer every call. In the weeks after, Geo-Fencing can show your ads in the neighborhoods the storm hit while homeowners compare roofers.

How do I get more roof replacement jobs, not just repairs?

Reach homeowners while they're researching a new roof, then stay in front of them while they decide. Google PPC targets searches like "roof replacement estimate," and Meta Ads show your before-and-after projects to homeowners in your service area. Reviews that mention full replacements also help homeowners trust you with the bigger job.

Can you guarantee a certain number of roofing leads?

No. Nobody controls Google rankings or how many homeowners search in a given month. What we do offer: for Google Business Profile Management and advertising campaigns, including Local Services Ads and Geo-Fencing, if you're not satisfied at the end of the service period, we extend the campaign at no charge. This applies when onboarding was completed and approvals came in on time, you raise the concern at the end of the initial period, and your account is in good standing.

Google PPC and Meta Ads are planned with you on a strategy call, and your ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta.
