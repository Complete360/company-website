Information We Collect
3.1 — Information You Provide Directly

We collect personal information you voluntarily submit when you interact with our website or services, including:

  • Contact forms: Name, phone number, email address, and message content.

When you visit our website, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, referral URLs, and navigation paths.
  • Location data: General geographic location inferred from your IP address (city/region level — not precise GPS location).
  • Cookie data: Preferences and session data stored via cookies. See Section 5 for full details.
3.3 — Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party sources, including:

5
Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, pixel tags, and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our website, understand how visitors interact with our content, and measure the performance of our advertising campaigns.

5.1 — Types of Cookies We Use
  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly (e.g., session management, form security). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site — which pages are most visited, how long people stay, and where they came from.
  • Advertising cookies: Used to track conversions from our Google and Meta ad campaigns and to serve you relevant ads after visiting our site.
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences and settings to improve your experience on return visits.

We use the following third-party tracking tools on our website:

Google Analytics
Tracks website traffic, user behavior, and session data to help us improve site performance.
Google Ads (Tag)
Measures ad conversions and enables remarketing to prior visitors through Google's ad network.
Meta Pixel
Tracks visitor actions and ad conversions on Facebook and Instagram. Enables custom audience targeting.
5.2 — Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. You can also opt out of Google's advertising tracking at adssettings.google.com and Meta's tracking via your Facebook Privacy Settings.

SMS & Email Communications
6.1 — Complete360's Own Communications

By submitting your contact information through our website or forms, you may receive communications from us including service updates, promotional offers, appointment reminders, and follow-up messages via email, SMS, or phone.

  • Email: We comply with the CAN-SPAM Act. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Transactional emails (receipts, service confirmations) may still be sent after unsubscribing from marketing.
  • SMS/Text: We comply with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). By providing your phone number and agreeing to receive texts, you consent to receive automated text messages from us. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • Opt-out: You may opt out of all marketing communications at any time by replying STOP to any text, clicking unsubscribe in any email, or calling us at (866) 527-6205.
6.2 — Client Use of the CRM Platform

Complete360 provides a CRM platform that enables our clients to send their own email and SMS marketing communications to their customers. When clients use our platform for this purpose:

  • Client responsibility: Our clients are solely responsible for obtaining proper consent from their own contacts before sending marketing messages, in accordance with TCPA, CAN-SPAM, and any other applicable laws.
  • Data processing: We process contact data on behalf of our clients as a service provider. We do not use client contact lists for our own marketing purposes.
  • Compliance tools: Our platform includes built-in unsubscribe and opt-out management tools. Clients are required to use these features appropriately.
  • Abuse policy: We reserve the right to suspend platform access for any client found to be using our system to send unsolicited, deceptive, or illegal communications.

If you are a recipient of a message sent by a Complete360 client through our platform and wish to opt out, please use the unsubscribe link included in any email or reply STOP to any text. If you need further assistance, contact us at (866) 527-6205.

  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email or prominent notice on our website if such a transfer occurs.
  • With your consent: We may share your information for any other purpose with your explicit prior consent.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

  • Active clients: Account and service data is retained for the duration of your engagement with us and for a reasonable period afterward for accounting and legal purposes.
  • CRM platform data: Upon cancellation of your CRM subscription, your data remains accessible for 30 days to allow for export. After 30 days, data may be permanently deleted.
  • Website visitors: Analytics and cookie data is retained per the data retention settings of the applicable platform (typically 14–26 months for Google Analytics).
  • Payment records: Transaction records are retained for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with standard accounting and tax requirements.
