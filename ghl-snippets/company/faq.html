Paid Advertising

Local Service Ads are Google's pay-per-lead ad product that appears at the very top of search results — above regular Google Ads. When your business is approved, you receive a green "Google Guaranteed" checkmark badge that signals to customers that Google has verified your business, license, and insurance. You only pay when a customer contacts you directly through the ad. We handle the full setup, verification, and ongoing management starting at $499 setup.

Geo-Fencing lets you draw a virtual boundary around any physical location — a neighborhood, a zip code, a competitor's parking lot, or a specific street — and serve ads to anyone who enters that zone on their mobile device or connected TV (streaming apps). It's hyper-local targeting with no wasted spend on people outside your service area. We offer three tiers — Starter, Growth, and Pro — depending on your market size and goals.

Google PPC targets people based on what they search for. Geo-Fencing targets people based on where they physically are or have been. They work differently and often complement each other:

  • Google PPC: Best for capturing people actively searching for your service right now.
  • Geo-Fencing: Best for brand awareness and targeting within a specific geographic area — including competitor locations and your own service territory.

Many clients run both for maximum coverage — capturing high-intent search traffic with PPC and staying top-of-mind in their local area with Geo-Fencing.

Connected TV (CTV) refers to streaming devices and smart TVs — Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, Hulu, and similar platforms. Our Geo-Fencing program includes CTV ad placements, so your video ad can appear in streaming apps on the TVs of people who have entered your geo-fenced area. It's like local TV advertising but targeted to your exact geographic audience without the massive cost of traditional broadcast.
